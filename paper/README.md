# Paper status

`draft.md` is preserved as a historical research draft. The complete raw
archive behind its original tables was not retained in the current append-only
format, so those historical numeric claims cannot be regenerated independently.
It is intentionally excluded from CommCanary release distributions under
[ADR 0008](../docs/adr/0008-paper-publication-boundary.md).

New publication material must come from the manifest-driven experiment
pipeline: immutable attempts, explicit selection, a complete verdict, and the
canonical `aggregate.json`, `aggregate.csv`, and `paper-fragment.md` emitted by
`experiments/rostam/analyze.py`. No hand-copied headline value is a substitute
for those generated bytes.

The current manifest-driven evidence is published under
[`experiments/rostam/results/`](../experiments/rostam/results/README.md),
including the complete r1/r2/r3 decision-gate history, normalized raw archive,
byte-generated paper fragment, and four-state policy verdict. The r3 exact-work
gate reached 26/28 pair agreement and Kendall tau-b 0.857, but its frozen
outcome is `inconclusive` because uncertainty and stability checks failed.

That evidence does not retroactively validate the older tables in `draft.md`.
Section 5.10 records the new result for context; the generated r3 fragment and
verdict remain authoritative for a replacement paper.
